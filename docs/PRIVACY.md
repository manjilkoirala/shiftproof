# ShiftProof privacy policy — release draft

ShiftProof stores the jobs, working hours, rates, notes and employer records you enter locally on your device. It does not require an account or send those records to a ShiftProof server. The app contains no advertising or analytics integrations.

When you export a backup or report, you choose where to save or share it. The recipient or storage service you select handles that file under its own policies. Exported files contain your work and pay information and are not encrypted by ShiftProof.

You can delete individual shifts within the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its local records. Keep a backup before doing so if you want to retain your history. Files you have exported remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately.

The app does not request access to your location, camera or contacts. File access is used to import backups and export reports through the system picker and share sheet. Android automatic app-data backup is disabled; backups are manual.

The publisher must add their legal name, a working privacy contact and an effective date before publishing this policy. This draft describes the intended production app; Expo development tooling is separate.
